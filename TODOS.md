# Roadmap and open work

> Maintainer planning source. Last reviewed: 2026-10-29.

## Current roadmap

The approved product scope is to operate and maintain the existing snapshot-based coverage described in [README.md](README.md) and [the coverage audit](docs/INDIA_COURT_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md). No discrete feature or court-geography expansion is currently approved for implementation.

The [long-term data strategy](docs/LONG_TERM_DATA_STRATEGY.md) and [metric strategy](docs/METRIC_STRATEGY.md) describe future directions and candidate metrics. They are strategic options, not approved implementation tasks. New source classes, metric families, or deeper coverage require a separate decision and must pass the source, methodology, public-trust, and operational checks in [the expansion gates](docs/MULTI_STATE_EXPANSION_GATES.md).

## Open work

No discrete, approved implementation tasks are currently recorded. Add a task here only when it has an owner or clear next action, a reason it is in scope, and a verifiable completion condition. Keep release evidence in the operator ledgers rather than duplicating it here:

- `docs/internal/DEPLOYMENT_STATUS.md`
- `docs/internal/RELEASE_HISTORY.md`
- `docs/internal/EXPANSION_REVIEW_LOG.md`

High Court public-beta additions must continue to use deliberate, documented batches unless a concrete operational blocker requires an exception; record the reason with that change. This is a standing release rule, not an open expansion task.
